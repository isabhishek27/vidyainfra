About Vidya Infra

Where architectural vision meets construction discipline.

Vidya Infra Construction is a full-service infrastructure and design solutions provider dedicated to shaping functional, high-quality spaces. We bring a unified approach to the built environment, seamlessly integrating structural integrity, modern aesthetics, and mechanical precision.

From full-scale architectural design and custom interior execution to critical MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing) installations and long-term civil maintenance, we manage every phase of a project’s lifecycle. Our focus remains on delivering durable, cost-effective, and precisely engineered solutions tailored to commercial, industrial, and residential needs.
